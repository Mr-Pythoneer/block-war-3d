Money

Money is kills. Mini zombies pay 1, mini zombies with helmets 2, normal zombies 4. Shoot gold ore for 100–250. You start with 500. Reach 1000 to win, then keep going.

Pickups

  • Apple: +1 HP. Golden apple: +5 HP.
  • Green laser wall: +2 HP every second you stand in it (up to 30 HP).
  • Red laser wall: hurts you, but shreds zombies. Lure them in.

Bosses

Snow Boss35 s into a round. Throws snowballs that knock you back and leaves a snow trail.
Wall Boss100 s in. Raises stone walls that block you and your bullets.
Flame Boss200 s in. Clears the horde, burns on touch, throws fireballs.
Glide BossShows up if a round starts while you have exactly 300–305 money.

The shop is locked while a boss is alive. Beating any boss starts a new round (other bosses stay). TNT, the turret and the sniper hit bosses 5 times harder.

Shop

Opening the shop ends the round and the horde retreats. Closing it starts a new round: +4 HP (if the round lasted at least 20 s) and the boss timers restart. Some upgrades make the horde bigger, and so does surviving: more zombies at 3, 6 and 10 minutes.

New stuff

SplitterBig orange block. Breaks into 3 minis when it dies.
BoomerRed, with TNT on its chest. Blows up next to you. Shoot it when it's near other zombies.
SpitterYellow. Stays back and spits goo that slows you down.
Shield guyBlocks bullets from the front. Go around it, or use TNT, mines, the sniper or the laser gun.
Block KingBeat 3 bosses and he comes 20 s into the next round. Drops blocks where the red squares are and calls minis.

New zombies start showing up the longer you survive.
