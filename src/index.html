Money

Money is kills: 2 to 6 a block, more for the tough ones. Shoot gold ore for 100–250 (yellow on the map). You start with 500. The game never ends; see how long you last.

Pickups

  • Apple: +1 HP. Golden apple: +5 HP.
  • Green laser wall: +2 HP every second you stand in it (up to 30 HP).
  • Red laser wall: hurts you, but shreds blocks. Lure them in.

Bosses

Snow Boss35 s into a round. Throws snowballs that knock you back and leaves a snow trail.
Wall Boss100 s in. Raises stone walls that block you and your bullets.
Flame Boss200 s in. Clears the horde, burns on touch, throws fireballs.
Glide BossShows up if a round starts while you have exactly 300–305 money.

You can shop during boss fights; the bosses wait for you. Beating any boss starts a new round (other bosses stay). TNT, the turret and the sniper hit bosses 5 times harder.

Shop

Opening the shop ends the round and the horde retreats. Closing it starts a new round: +4 HP (if the round lasted at least 20 s) and the boss timers restart. Some upgrades make the horde bigger, and so does surviving: more blocks at 3, 6 and 10 minutes.

New stuff

SplitterBig orange block made of smaller blocks. Breaks into 3 minis when it dies.
BoomerA TNT block with a face. Blows up next to you. Shoot it when it's near other blocks.
SpitterA slime cube. Stays back and spits goo that slows you down.
Shield guyA grey block pushing a wall. Blocks bullets from the front. Go around it, or use TNT, mines, the sniper or the laser gun.
Block KingBeat 3 bosses and he comes 20 s into the next round. Drops blocks where the red squares are and calls minis.

New kinds of blocks start showing up the longer you survive.
